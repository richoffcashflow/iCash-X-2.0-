# DealMachine delayed-check handoff

On November 2, the live system had 93 free people lists that were still marked waiting after cleanup deleted them, compared with 39 completed positive checks and four verified empty checks. Forty-one waiting checks had a later list for the same primary property; 21 had a later smaller batch. Candidate selection was recomputed on each delayed ticket, so new candidates and independently issued sibling tickets could change the exact batch fingerprint before its free count ran.

The compatible SQL migration keeps a recent, unchanged preflight's original property cohort when its leader resumes. Other batches and new ticket selection skip its siblings before their limits. Creation rechecks overlapping cohorts under the existing account advisory lock and shares the ticket issuer's short budget-row lock. No lock spans a provider request. A ticket that wins first keeps its own work; a list that wins first keeps its siblings together.

The five-minute selection preference ends on changed source, manual takeover, a different activity/mobile pool, a separately issued sibling ticket, started owner work, unresolved-owner fences or expiry. Lower funds cannot truncate the original list into a different purchase. Normal candidate eligibility and the final atomic paid admission remain authoritative. Counted positive evidence remains attached to the exact batch even after normal list cleanup. Stale and failed free receipts are not erased, reset or interpreted as empty.

This improves the handoff; it does not resolve DealMachine's recurring HTTP 500 or add automatic capacity recovery. Every later unknown export still needs its own explicit review. The reviewed request `req_017c683cfcc54ad6` returned no export ID or credit usage. Its separate free capacity review succeeded at 14:01:05 UTC with a complete idle inventory of 61 entries. Only scheduling capacity was released; the original receipt, owner fence and financial holds remain. Ordinary exports subsequently completed, but this is not proof that the provider failure is fixed.

Validation covers expanded/shrinking candidate sets, competing overlapping cohorts, racing sibling ticket issuance, source/account/pool changes, manual controls, reduced funds, exact positive receipts, cleanup, expiry and function privileges. Native PostgreSQL exercises real concurrent sessions. All tests are isolated and make no paid provider requests.
