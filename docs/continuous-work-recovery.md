# Continuous work after an export failure

The November 2 production audit found funded, unpaused customer accounts with available credits and an organization-wide contact-export hold after a completed DealMachine HTTP 500. Property searches continued, but no later contact export could start. Changing a spending percentage would not remove that hold: funded-wallet admission already permits continuous work.

Recent successful provider responses also confirmed a higher organization request entitlement: 70/minute and 10,000/day. The separate `verified-dealmachine-capacity.sql` migration requires fresh matching original observations before moving the application from 50/4,900 to 60/9,800. It preserves counters and window starts, retains provider backoff, and saves the source evidence with the original configuration privately. It does not buy a plan, enable overages or alter customer prices.

The owner requested automatic recovery. `automatic-export-capacity.sql` adds an initially disabled private policy. The existing authenticated maintenance lane can claim a recovery for an exact completed `internal_error` response, with a saved provider request ID but no export ID. Timeouts, missing responses, incomplete request observations and explicit manual reviews retain their original holds.

Each attempt permits at most three free GETs: a nonempty recent unfiltered organization inventory, then separate pending and processing inventories. Both active-status results must be completely empty. All requests consume shared quota, save their original raw receipts privately and have independent one-use claims. This works when historical inventory exceeds 100 exports; it does not mistake a truncated all-status page for a complete inventory. The unfiltered page must include a terminal record at least as recent as the incident, with provider whole-second timestamp precision. No history row is attributed to the failed purchase by time.

Successful verification releases only the organization scheduling slot. The failed owners remain excluded across accounts; their purchase, unknown costs and wallet commitments remain unchanged. Unrelated eligible production work can then proceed through its ordinary final admission. No purchase, call, text, download or development simulation is performed by recovery.

Failed and interrupted free checks create distinct attempts after 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 and then 60 minutes. Prior claims and responses remain immutable. Saving identical evidence or confirming the final result may retry after acknowledgment loss; provider GET claims are never reused. Each attempt records the executing release. The existing export monitoring continues to flag prolonged blockage.

Deploy the migration disabled, publish the matching application, require exact-commit CI including native PostgreSQL contention, then enable the private policy. Rollback disables the policy; it does not erase evidence or revoke a verified historical capacity decision. New releases inherit the policy through the established production release gate rather than requiring an incident-by-incident approval.

Offline checks cover source/HTTP/identity requirements, stale and malformed inventories, more than 100 historical exports, active exports, missing quota, interrupted requests, acknowledgment loss, backoff, private grants, and unchanged owner/financial holds. Native PostgreSQL runs multiple competing claim, save and finalize sessions. These checks establish recovery behavior, not provider reliability or guaranteed customer spending.

Other observed constraints remain real: the 424 sender is disabled, old contact checks expire, and uncertain calls/messages retain their original reconciliation requirements. Continuous operation means selecting eligible work and recovering safe failures; it does not override pauses, recipient controls, available credits or provider limits.

## Gateway failure and completed-search recovery

The later November 2 audit found a new completed HTTP 504 gateway response occupying the global export slot, blocking contact enrichment for 14 otherwise eligible stalled accounts. `gateway-export-capacity.sql` adds a separately disabled `gateway_enabled` switch and `gateway-idle-v1` policy. It requires the original admitted, account/operation-bound owner lookup to have finished at least five minutes ago, the exact saved DealMachine Cloudflare gateway HTML, an unchanged receipt, an unexpired original hold with no known export/CSV/recovery, and no active local lease or other blocking export. It reuses all three free inventory checks, provider quota admission, immutable attempts and bounded backoff. Only organization capacity can be released; the original owners and customer money remain held.

Three other funded accounts had current property searches with completed HTTP 500 receipts but no exclusion list. The original continuation handled only unknown exclusion-list requests, so these exact failures froze otherwise available independent research. `failed-search-continuity.sql` adds a disabled `completed_error_continuation_enabled` switch. With it enabled, the existing continuation accepts these finished error receipts and null-list configurations while retaining aged request proof, exact query claims, original rate/configuration snapshots, permanent original-ZIP fences, saved market restrictions, affordability, account locking, Pause checks and late original receipt recovery. It never retries the failed search or releases its money.

Install both migrations disabled. Require the exact release's full reliability checks and native export contention for both the original 500 and new 504 cases, then verify matching web/worker releases before activating the private switches. Rollback disables those switches without editing source records or historical capacity/continuation decisions. Offline cases cover incomplete/recent/mismatched requests, other HTTP outcomes, active leases, known recovery, active provider exports, Pause, old-policy compatibility, immutable original operations, idempotency and private grants.

Older call, text and legacy-search receipts remain separate investigations. A completed conversation or zero-duration quota rejection alone is not evidence of zero total provider cost. Never fabricate a USD observation, waive an unknown commitment, replay a call or count receipt polling as completed work.

## Expired free-check renewal

After the gateway recovery succeeded at 22:31 UTC, all five repaired search accounts completed new research by 22:33. The original five operations retained their exact hashes. The export recovery saved three HTTP 200 receipts, with 225 historical exports and zero pending or processing exports; the original failed purchase and money remained held. Both production hosts ran `9e6c1c5ba4550cc858a1704674f79fc1aff12a97`, whose 671 suites, browser checks and native contention checks passed.

Further investigation found 116 expired positive contact checks across 43 still-stalled accounts. Their fingerprint was unique forever, while positive evidence expired after 30 minutes. The unchanged next attempt therefore returned held indefinitely. Other abandoned free lists had already been deleted but retained the same permanent cache key.

`owner-preflight-renewal.sql` installs a disabled renewal switch and immutable generations. An expired check can have one successor only after its old provider list has confirmed cleanup and its local handler window has ended. Positive evidence retains its 30-minute validity; known-empty evidence retains its 24-hour exclusion. Unconfirmed list creation/deletion cannot renew. A new generation uses a distinct list name, lease and empty receipt set, waits the full 90-second indexing interval, and must obtain fresh exact baseline and eligible-owner counts before the unchanged paid admission permits anything.

Renewal rechecks account Pause after the shared budget lock, membership, configuration, rates, funds, exact current screening, manual property control, phone pool and unknown owner purchases. Existing cohort/sibling arbitration and shared provider quota remain in force. Old records are never reset or deleted, and superseded failures remain in private history while current attempts own the active finding. Deploy disabled, require matching release checks including native competing-renewal and Pause transactions, then activate; rollback disables renewal without altering history or original purchases.

Older billing holds are not repaired by this renewal. Missing original provider charges remain explicit unresolved work, and the critical incident must not close merely because the release or free checks succeeded.
