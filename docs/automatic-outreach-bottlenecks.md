# Automatic outreach routing and recovery

The November 13 top-account investigation reproduced three failures: a first seller text could select an SMS-only number and prevent its later call, a first call could select a voice-only number and prevent its follow-up text, and a never-admitted call could remain held after a transient business-number verification failure. Ten same-day mismatches across two inspected accounts were bound to SMS-only conversations; these were not exhausted voice concurrency.

For a new conversation with current seller voice evidence and an account whose calling channel is current, first SMS allocation now requires an enabled SMS sender with verified outbound calling and forwarding. Conversely, a first call with a current operational SMS target and enabled texting requires an SMS-enabled caller. This retains deterministic balancing and the existing sender-health exclusion. Existing threads and original call identities remain binding; the repair neither rotates them nor enables any number. Contacts without current evidence for the other channel retain their existing allocation behavior.

The existing maintenance recovery can now revisit `business_number_mismatch` and `business_number_verification_required` only when the exact conversation currently resolves to a usable calling number. It retains the reviewed-agent check, original budget lock, membership, Pause, wallet, fresh financial snapshot, current contact evidence, hours, recipient spacing, capacity and queue-slot checks. Normal dispatch repeats actual provider verification and all final admission checks.

Recovery requires one closed matching original ticket, no operation key, no supplier call or conversation identifier, no operation spend, no recording or live conversation, and no other queued work for that recipient. Callbacks, requested SMS calls and Meta sequences remain with their own recovery rules. The original ticket is unchanged, and the append-only recovery receipt permits at most one recovery per job across all supported preflight failures. Unknown outcomes and expired evidence remain held.

The CLI-generated migration is compatible with the existing five-argument maintenance call. Install it through the migration service only after reviewing the diff and passing its exact-commit checks. Both function replacements require the captured predecessor hashes and run atomically with the audit constraint and partial index. There is no provider, pricing, UI or credit-limit change.

Validation uses the captured production functions in isolated PostgreSQL fixtures. It reproduces all three failures and verifies route continuity in both channel orders, source binding, sender health, suppression, expiry, original tickets and spending, private ACLs, and the 20-job bound. Native PostgreSQL races additionally verify one recovery across concurrent maintenance, recipient deduplication, and serialized Pause, wallet reservation, caller disable, STOP and unknown admission changes. No paid calls, texts or research are used for development tests.

This repair does not manufacture fresh contact evidence or make a phone-less property callable. The existing contact supply and quota-pacing paths still govern how many fresh callable properties arrive. Production measurement must distinguish a queued recovery from an actual accepted call.
