# Automatic seller draft preparation

Completed, provider-bound seller conversations now trigger internal draft assistance when reconciliation saves the verified call result. Reconciliation can retry draft assistance independently; its failure does not undo the saved conversation or its billing.

The worker fills only empty fields in one existing unsigned draft, using exact seller statements from saved call transcripts and received texts. It preserves existing edits, provenance and proposed-price status. Conflicting statements, refusals, multiple seller identities or history exceeding the safe inspection bound require review. Paused accounts, manual-control properties, opt-outs, human requests and existing signing envelopes are left unchanged. The final write compares the original terms and timestamp, and the existing sent-contract database freeze remains enforced.

This prepares information; it does not establish ownership, all required signers, agreement to missing terms or signing authority. It does not send a DocuSeal request, charge for signing, text a new contract, grant credits or sign for anyone. Original quotes remain in the existing conversation records and the workspace's preparation evidence view.

## Remaining delivery dependency

The voice contract-text tool still requires a matching, approved pending signing envelope. The current first-call workflow does not create a new binding agreement solely from a model-provided price or a verbal yes. Legal names, all required owners/signers, legal description and actual agreed terms must be available and reviewed before the existing confirmed signing action. Seller and customer signatures remain separately verified by the provider.

## Validation

The focused suite checks exact source statements, refusal and conflicting-party holds, control settings, existing envelopes and edits, preservation of provenance, bounded history, compare-and-set contention and completed-call integration. Tests use synthetic data and no paid provider operations.

## Negotiation ceiling

The calculated cash offer is the private maximum, not an opening quote or draft purchase price. The seller call first learns the seller's desired price, condition and timing, then seeks a lower amount within authority without a fabricated fixed discount or invented market evidence. Existing agreed prices are preserved. Draft creation and preparation no longer fill a blank price from the calculated ceiling.
