# VIP and daily prepaid spending

Owner approved 2026-10-07. Final pricing supersedes the earlier 2x proposal:

- Standard $50/month; VIP $100/month total.
- Standard-to-VIP costs exactly $50 once for the current paid period; same renewal date.
- VIP renewals use the existing subscription item, not a second subscription. Existing retention discounts keep their original remaining term.
- Downgrading changes the next renewal to $50 without proration or a charge today. Paid VIP access stays until the recorded VIP paid-through date. Restoring VIP renewal during that period is free.
- VIP receives 20% off all credit-funded backend activity: AI work and purchased leads. AI usage is 2.4x versus Standard 3x; lead prices are 80% of the Standard lead price, rounded up to whole cents. Apply the discount once per action. Credit purchases remain dollar-for-dollar; the subscription price is unchanged.
- Eligible, funded VIP accounts rank ahead of Standard in new seller lead allocation. Preserve the deployed any-market allocation; existing consent, budget, and assignment exclusions still apply.
- VIP can rename its bot. Existing names remain; new Standard bots use iCash X. VIP now also includes one private custom voice from an uploaded recording, used on outbound seller and buyer calls. The shared inbound reception voice is unchanged. Inactive-account lead takeover is not included.

The daily allowance is half the balance available at the day's start (initialized before the first reservation), bounded by a lower saved daily limit. It resets at midnight America/Chicago. Outstanding work from a previous day remains funded but does not consume the new day's allowance twice. All new reservations share the atomic wallet gate. Additional funding after the snapshot is available through the explicit allowance override; first funding after a zero balance initializes half automatically.

“Allow more spending today” sets an absolute extra allowance, so repeated clicks cannot add it twice. It cannot exceed existing available credits, charge a card, revive a cancelled subscription, or unpause a user-paused bot. Next day the override is zero.

Workspace questions use the configured gpt-4.1-mini model. Reserve 5 cents before dispatch, settle actual input/cached/output token costs at 3x or VIP 2.4x, and carry fractions of cents across usage rather than charging a penny minimum per answer. Token prices checked against https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-4.1-mini on 2026-10-07: $0.40 / $0.10 / $1.60 per million. Requests without verified provider usage do not charge the customer. Pure local fallback has no AI charge.
