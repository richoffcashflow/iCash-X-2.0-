## Reply corrections

- Slow-work and no-interest complaints take priority over incidental references to the old version, buyers or phone settings. Short frustration replies stay attached to that issue; acknowledgments do not trigger another troubleshooting loop.
- Automatic seller calling and texting are distinguished from the browser Call button. Replies explain recorded overall activity and verified blockers, or clearly state when the cause is unknown. Run and Searching for a deal do not prove active tasks, recent outreach or interested sellers. A later customer Pause does not explain earlier missing work and is never reversed by chat.
- Outbound identity comes from service-connected business numbers selected by the server. Settings holds the customer's contact number, not a caller-ID selector or bring-your-own-number control. A location answer to an earlier incorrect setup question gets a correction rather than unrelated market-selection guidance. Exact caller ID remains unverified unless supported by the actual call record.
- Missing-contact wording includes ordinary contractions and pronoun follow-ups. The reply keeps the issue open and does not promise that numbers will arrive. A request for the next step asks for the affected property instead of repeating account totals.
- More work credits do not guarantee contracts, improve the strategy or fix technical problems. VIP does not remove required reviews, signatures, title verification and closing participation.
- Follow-ups preserve trusted operator findings about conversation visibility and unfinished charges without claiming those findings explain seller interest. A verified repair is not treated as an unresolved operator finding.
- A recorded software payment, inactive access and disputed cancellation remain under review. Replies do not invent the cancellation actor, override saved cancellation/access terms or recommend a second payment or restart as troubleshooting. A genuinely new cancellation or course question retains its own flow.

Known current-step cases use deterministic replies before optional model generation. Product-conflict checks reject invented caller-ID setup and guaranteed later contact information.
