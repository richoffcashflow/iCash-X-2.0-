# Support question recognition

The November 12 support audit reproduced three answer-routing failures with fictional conversations:

- A missing seller-call recording received unrelated account-wide outreach counts. Call audio now has a shared account/guest reply path, distinguishes capability from available audio, retains an unresolved playback issue through short follow-ups, and asks for the affected property, call time and recording status. Repeated failed playback enters the existing management-review workflow. Live microphone problems, custom voice, voicemail classification and recorded-attempt statistics keep their existing routes.
- Complaints that a bot stopped without being paused, appears on without running, or shows only older leads could fall through to unsupported explanations about eligible inventory. These now use the existing account checks, distinguish overall historical activity from current work, and retain short answers to matching questions. Unknown checks remain unknown; Pause and operator/topic boundaries are preserved.
- Combined deletion and stop-renewal requests could answer only deletion. Natural requests to stop billing now retain the existing subscription-cancellation instructions alongside deletion review. Failed sign-in does not trigger another sign-in instruction. The reply does not cancel, delete, refund or change any account.

Source checks: `components/call-recording.tsx` enumerates available, pending, absent, expired and failed audio states; `components/property-call-history.tsx` keeps recordings independent of transcripts. Subscription cancellation retains the existing confirmation flow. The customer interface and production controls are unchanged.

Regression suites: `support-recording-question`, `support-stopped-work-phrasing`, and `support-deletion-renewal`. Each failed on the original main behavior and is registered with the release verifier. All fixtures are fictional and forbid paid model requests. Exact-head canonical checks and verified production deployment remain required before claiming live behavior. An answer correction does not recover a recording, resume stalled work or establish customer-confirmed resolution.

Additional fictional replays preserve a recording capability follow-up that omits “call” only inside the current recording episode. Operator changes, success and other topics stop inheritance. This incorporates the distinct follow-up coverage from PR #693 into this shared handler.

Guest paid-purchase wording no longer captures an explicit refund/cancellation request. Sales-disclosure complaints about separate work credits retain the actual refund concern for management review without approving a refund or changing policy. Duplicate-charge and cancellation routes retain priority. Replacement legacy-offer link requests answer the identified step without inventing eligibility, issuing a link or repeating page-identification questions. These corrections do not repair access or checkout. Coverage: `tests/support-refund-offer-followup.test.mjs`.

A saved no-progress health flag must not erase completed research or outreach already present in the same account evidence. Shared activity and confirmation replies now preserve those overall counts and describe the flag as a delay check, without inferring current work, its cause or a restart. Unknown outreach evidence cannot supply counts or a blockage claim. Fictional replays in `tests/support-progress-evidence.test.mjs` cover positive and zero history, unknown checks, Pause, pending work, guest context, operator findings and topic changes. This corrects support interpretation only; it does not restart work or establish recovery.

Explicit requests for a support reference or email during management review now answer with the server-bound conversation ID and the established support inbox. Neither is described as a separate ticket, direct human assignment or promised follow-up. Account and guest routes supply only their already-authorized conversation ID; customer text and older replies cannot supply it. Billing actions and seller/buyer questions keep their separate routes. Fictional coverage: `tests/support-case-reference.test.mjs` and the existing route tests.

An explicit business calling-number error now stays with the calling service instead of being interpreted as a missing seller contact. The error explains an unavailable/unverified outbound number without diagnosing an account or recommending personal phone edits. Conditional questions about pausing a bot that is not working receive the existing Pause guidance while preserving started work, subscription terms and unresolved failures. Fictional coverage: `support-calling-pause-boundary`; no call is retried and no account control changes.
