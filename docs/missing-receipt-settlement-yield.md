# Missing research receipts and settlement contention

An unchanged research original without a saved receipt cannot settle. The existing estimate adapter nevertheless took the shared operating-budget row before finding that the receipt was missing. The November 16 read-only investigation counted 1,145 pending owner-enrichment originals and 21 pending property searches without their matching receipt rows; 522 had been checked in the preceding fifteen minutes. This identifies unnecessary contention, not proof that those original financial holds may be cleared.

The guarded migration adds one refusal before that lock in `icash_settle_estimate_before_sms_tokens(text)`. It applies only to a still-dispatched research operation whose own source receipt is absent. Search and owner receipts must match operation and account; buyer receipts must match the operation and its deal's account. A receipt from another account or a different source remains insufficient. Missing rates, other operation kinds, non-dispatched states and every receipt-present case retain the complete previous path, including the final global budget lock, original-operation lock, receipt reread, observed credits, costs and ledger admission.

The early result remains `false` (held). It does not settle, release money, change customer status, reset an operation, replay a request or change recovery cadence. The ordinary collector still advances `estimate_checked_at`. A receipt that commits after the absence check is handled by the next ordinary receipt-driven or scheduled recovery. A receipt removed or changed after a positive optimistic read cannot authorize settlement: the unchanged locked adapter reads it again.

The CLI-generated migration pins target source MD5 `d463afb80ef551b673a1f7c43710117f` and downstream research-settlement MD5 `a3ffd9bc1ecd0eab2c1eff5a59727ea9`. The verified rewritten target is `14338cfa206754da98f675ff433e1755`. It takes shared transaction advisory lock `726341927` before source validation, with three-second lock and fifteen-second statement deadlines, then verifies the exact replacement and all unchanged `pg_proc` metadata. Owner, invoker mode, empty search path, volatility and private ACL remain unchanged. The inspected current source has no active manifest pin for this target; the earlier acquisition-isolation migration contains a historical prerequisite check only. The outer contact-refresh and SMS wrappers remain unchanged, including the owner-only intermediate wrapper and service-only SECURITY DEFINER entry point.

PGlite exercises all three source bindings, unknown/malformed/mismatched evidence, normal retry cursor advancement, reserved/cancelled/settled and other-kind behavior, original-credit settlement, idempotency, unchanged financial/source bytes, role restrictions, metadata, migration rollback and drift refusal. Native PostgreSQL reproduces the old missing-receipt timeout behind the budget row, verifies prompt held results for absent and cross-account receipts, and retains lock waits for other kinds/states. It also races an uncommitted receipt, its later committed settlement and deletion while waiting on the budget, and rejects concurrent migration replay. These are synthetic fixtures; the foundation ledger models existing settlement effects and is not a production ledger replacement.
