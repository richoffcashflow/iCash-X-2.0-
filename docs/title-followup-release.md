# Automatic title follow-up dispatch

The existing Railway polling worker can now receive a `title_followup` capability from Supabase and dispatch it through the existing Vercel internal automation endpoint. One ticket per minute globally prevents follow-up work from starving other jobs. No new worker deployment is required by the protocol. Feature scheduling is disabled during rollout and enabled only after the Vercel handler is ready.

One automatic email per title-opening request, due two weekdays after the original was accepted by Resend. No repeated chase sequence or attachment resends. The original request's verified recipient, rate and subject reference are reused. A verified inbound reply cancels pending follow-up. Pause, manual takeover, task dismissal/completion, inactive deal stages, invalid or expired contact verification, test/unsigned contracts, rate expiry, wallet limits, daily limits, margin admission and company cash protection are checked before dispatch. Reservation and one-use claim share a transaction, so failed admission cannot strand a new reservation. Calls already claimed/in flight cannot be recalled by a later Pause or reply.

The existing title_email rate applies (currently 25 customer credit cents); actual usage reconciliation remains separate and unfinished at the broader platform level. This release never invents vendor invoices or marks reserved charges as settled costs. No operating cash or customer credits were added by deployment.

Missing email configuration and pre-send holds retry eligibility after six hours. Issued tasks that never reach claim may be reissued after fifteen minutes. Dispatched tasks never auto-retry; timeouts and uncertain provider acceptance require review, with reservations retained. Stable provider idempotency keys supplement the database claim. Provider acceptance is stored as a receipt, never treated as title opened, deposit paid or closing complete.

Required runtime setup: running Railway worker, RESEND_API_KEY, ICASH_TITLE_FROM_EMAIL on a verified sending domain, ICASH_TITLE_REPLY_EMAIL, valid title contact, signed production purchase and funded operating budget. Receiving uses the previously configured receiving webhook. No new provider account is introduced. The company operating budget remains disabled/unfunded unless explicitly funded separately.

Validation: mock denied/success/duplicate/timeout tests; rolled-back database tests for Pause, account isolation, test contracts, insufficient company funds, atomic reservation rollback, valid claim and duplicate claim; production build. No paid calls or outgoing emails were sent. A real full email round trip attached to an actual deal is still pending. Partner qualification automation and verified closing execution remain separate unfinished work.
