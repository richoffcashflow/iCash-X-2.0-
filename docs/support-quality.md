# Support improvement workflow

The owner requested a broader support improvement pass on October 30, 2026, using the support inbox as the source. Read-only review found recurring confusion about contact availability, contract timing, off-market properties, usage costs, failed offer checkout, and wrong checkout emails. Earlier automated replies sometimes answered a nearby topic or repeated a fallback without advancing the case.

The support conversation engine now has curated, offline answers for those recurring questions. A lead can precede a usable contact; a researched prospect does not establish seller interest; no verified average contract timeline is available; software access and prepaid work credits are separate. Checkout failure does not establish a code-delivery fault. Wrong-email correction requires purchase verification. A held call job is not evidence that a call reached a seller, and a repair time must not be invented. Actual failures remain open even when an adjacent feature question is answered.

**Review support quality** in `/support/admin` groups recent conversations by topic and identifies repeated replies, low-information replies, specific question/answer mismatches, repair claims needing verification, and open cases without an update for at least 24 hours. Each result opens the original conversation. A later operator correction supersedes earlier automatic replies for content flags. Closed and resolved cases are not labeled stalled.

The view pages through the unresolved backlog of all ages or conversations updated in the past seven days. Each immutable creation-time/ID page includes up to ten account and ten guest conversations, with up to 40 recent messages per case. Truncated histories and remaining pages are labeled. Counts describe the current page and review signals, not confirmed bug counts, resolution rates, or complete support coverage. A failed load shows an error rather than an empty healthy report.

The quality endpoint uses existing trusted-operator authorization and existing private support tables. It performs fixed, bounded reads only, redacts sensitive previews, escapes customer text, and returns `private, no-store`. It does not modify cases, send replies, change access, train on customer claims, or turn a review flag into repair evidence.

Use a recurring pattern to confirm the product fact or failure, add an anonymized regression replay, fix the relevant answer or product path, and verify it before describing it as repaired. Customer messages remain untrusted reports, not product policy or authorization. The tests exercise the actual conversation engine, report builder, collector, protected route and review navigation without real messages, provider activity or paid AI calls.

The November 8 continuous-improvement loop adds private priority candidates, deduplication keys, operator-correction signals and explicit customer-outcome counts to the same report. A separate hourly task carries verified failures through tests, fixes, automatic release and recurrence checks. The report itself remains read-only and does not train a model or mutate a case. See `docs/support-improvement-loop.md` for the controller, private checkpoint lease, release gates and limits.

Free-offer code complaints require identifying the checkout stage before asking for a payment receipt. The code may be requested by secure checkout, offer recovery or the sign-in page; entering details alone does not confirm a completed purchase. The shared account/guest route asks for that stage, retains failed attempts and leaves the issue open. Completed-checkout reports, operator corrections, billing requests and topic changes keep their separate routes. Fictional replays in `tests/support-checkout.test.mjs` cover these boundaries without sending codes, changing eligibility or contacting payment providers.

The October 31 review found bot-status complaints falling into sign-in help, repeated-question removal leaving only a thank-you and password warning, missing-number complaints answered with unrelated activity totals, and guest sign-in details resetting the issue. Customer accounts cannot add, upload or import their own leads on either plan. The answer now states that directly, covers a missing-number question in the same message, and never directs a customer to the owner's private demo form. No customer import endpoint was introduced; the existing demo endpoint remains owner-bound.

Bot-status complaints and short follow-ups use the saved account activity and call checks. Missing owner details do not establish a usable phone number; voicemail does not establish a seller response. Lead-arrival timing is distinct from outreach timing. Acknowledgments preserve unresolved failures, and code-delivery troubleshooting remembers both attempted channels, browser replies and payment-date/amount details without treating them as payment or identity verification. The quality view also flags orphaned warnings, bot/sign-in mismatches and import answers that fail to explain the restriction.

`tests/support-inbox-regressions.test.mjs` replays these cases with fictional account evidence and a transport that rejects paid model calls. It also covers topic changes, cancellation/payment priority, missing-number plus import questions, Standard/VIP boundaries, false model upload instructions and review signals. These checks verify support behavior, not a repair to a customer's underlying outreach or sign-in fault. Existing conversations, payment records, customer identities and provider operations remain unchanged by this release.

Missing automatic seller-text replies require property-bound conversation and sending evidence. The manual composer availability check is not proof of a worker delay or successful dispatch. Shared replies retain the unresolved issue through short accepted-check follow-ups, stop at operator/topic changes, and do not suggest a resolution note as a repair. Fictional coverage: `tests/support-seller-text-review.test.mjs`.

Checkout-email spelling corrections use the same ownership-review recognition in current-step routing and purchase guidance. A previously missing sign-in code must not override a newly explained email typo or trigger another request to the wrong address. Fictional account/guest and topic boundaries: `tests/support-checkout-email.test.mjs`. This does not verify a purchase, change an email or restore access.

An immediate access-timing question after an operator records an unresolved access review and no confirmed completion time preserves that finding even if the operator queue flag was cleared. The reply keeps the case in management review without inventing a deadline, restarting code troubleshooting or claiming restored access. Customer-supplied review claims, intervening topics and unrelated timing questions do not activate this path. Fictional account/guest replays: `tests/support-access-review-timing.test.mjs`.

Manual seller-contact questions after contact discovery must explain the existing Take over, Call and Texts controls instead of repeating contact-research timing. No message is sent by taking over; Return to bot restores eligible automatic follow-up. Missing-number, failed-contact, buyer/support and changed-topic questions retain their separate routes. Fictional replays: `tests/support-manual-contact.test.mjs`.

Reports that the AI is on but is not doing anything retain the missing-activity investigation. A direct running/green-light answer to a Run/Pause question does not restart generic troubleshooting. Current evidence remains authoritative; Run alone is not proof of outreach. Fictional topic-change boundaries: `tests/support-running-continuity.test.mjs`.

Past-tense reports that the bot did no work and immediate All activity/Your leads page answers retain the missing-activity review. Questions about recognizing activity use recorded evidence, without inferring a working bot or absent leads from a Run label. Explicit success, operator turns and topic changes stop inherited review. Thanks and side questions do not confirm an earlier activity fault is repaired. Fictional recurrence coverage: `tests/support-activity-recurrence.test.mjs`.
Credit-exhaustion questions distinguish new paid follow-up from work already started, including sellers already in conversation. An earlier payment is not unlimited follow-up credit. The shared account/guest answer retains credit, Run/Pause and other admission checks without asserting current balance, changing billing policy or declaring the case resolved. Fictional replays cover direct questions, immediate follow-ups, topic changes and intervening operator findings.

An earlier-work waiting complaint and its immediate home-page answer remain an activity review even after an old VIP-discount topic. Current account evidence determines whether costs are awaiting confirmation; the message does not establish final charges or a restart time.

A report that the app or workspace is running without movement, progress or activity enters the existing account activity review on the first turn. Run is not proof of completed work. Immediate page answers retain that investigation; browser/video questions, software-update questions, explicit success, operator findings, cancellation and topic changes keep their boundaries. Fictional replays extend `tests/support-activity-recurrence.test.mjs`.

Direct questions about whether the bot is really working use current recorded activity, including after unrelated side questions. A saved no-completed-work blockage stays under management review; Run alone cannot establish working automation or absent eligible inventory. Unknown evidence, explicit success and location/capability questions remain distinct. Fictional replays cover these boundaries without running providers.

Questions about pausing the bot or whether to buy VIP receive direct product guidance after a management handoff. Pause stops new work but does not prove a repair or erase already-started work and its costs. Standard includes ordinary automation and support; VIP is not a verified repair for missing activity. Short pause follow-ups bind only to an immediate bot-pause question, and purchase complaints retain their own route. Review explanations recognize missing-activity reports without overriding a later operator finding or explicit success. Fictional account/guest and topic boundaries are covered by `tests/support-review-guidance.test.mjs`; no account controls or billing are changed.

Lead-origin and requested-market questions receive the automatic-market explanation before generic lead-import or outreach summaries. No market change or cause is claimed without saved evidence; cases remain open. Fictional geography, billing/import and topic/operator boundaries are in `tests/support-location-guidance.test.mjs`.

Custom-voice setup guidance distinguishes uploading a recording to create a clone from playing a ready clone. Account and guest replies explain the existing own-voice consent and file requirements, preserve uncertain upload results, and stop inherited setup context at topic changes, operator findings or explicit success. A VIP check does not prove upload completion. Fictional replays in `tests/support-voice-setup.test.mjs` run without provider or paid model calls; no voice, consent, entitlement or customer account is changed.

Direct personal-phone and VIP-link questions use the existing Account details and VIP review controls, including mixed requests and punctuated VIP wording. Guest text does not verify a purchase or membership. Failed saves, billing complaints, unrelated numbers and topic changes retain their separate paths. `tests/support-account-details.test.mjs` uses fictional offline replays; this guidance never edits identity, purchases VIP or declares the underlying case resolved.

Bare complaints that the software is not what the customer purchased receive a focused question about the expected feature or service. A daily usage receipt cannot establish delivery of the disputed service. The shared account/guest route preserves specific faults, billing, cancellation, operator findings and topic changes, avoids repeating its clarification, and does not resolve the complaint from a thank-you. Fictional coverage is in `tests/support-purchase-complaint.test.mjs`; payment terms and customer records are unchanged.

A short confirmation that the receipt email matches retains the missing-code episode only after the immediate assistant receipt-email question. The statement does not verify a purchase or identity, restore access, or close the case. Other email subjects, operator turns, successful sign-in and topic changes retain their boundaries. Fictional coverage extends `tests/support-paid-access-audit.test.mjs`.
