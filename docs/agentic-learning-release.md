# Evidence-backed learning release

The November 3 owner approval ships the reviewed learning fixes. It does not authorize paid development calls, texts, exports or property searches. Normal production remains subject to the original financial, contact, suppression, provider-receipt and customer-control gates.

## Measurement and allocation

- Source-bound selling interest treats both reviewed cash and flexible-timing questions consistently. Ownership confirmation and a provider's unsupported `interested` flag are not selling interest. Original transcripts and provider results are never rewritten.
- Ranker SMS exposure uses the exact account-bound operation dispatch and provider receipt, not queue time. Label-independent age bands prevent recent volume from crowding out mature 30/60-day examples. Model qualification and influence remain unchanged.
- Evidence-v2 requires at least three contributing accounts and 100 resolved weighted trials per compared arm, complete positive settled costs, at least 10 positive outcomes (five verified contracts/closings), and a 95% lower-bound cost yield exceeding every competitor's upper bound by more than 5%. The most mature objective takes precedence even when it has zero successes. Local discovery uses the same evidence and comparison thresholds without pretending one account is three contributors.
- Stable account control groups remain exploratory. Eligible treatment allocations give a winner at most 60% of slots and retain challengers. Elevated opt-outs exclude an arm from exploration as well as learned allocation. An all-unsafe policy holds before provider or spend admission instead of falling through to an unsafe default.
- New identity-v5 seller texts preserve literal principal/address, the 160-character/GSM limits and footer-free format. Existing assignments, queued copy and uncertain sends are not reinterpreted. See [seller SMS learning](seller-sms-learning-v5.md).

These are conservative comparisons of observed outcomes per recorded cost. They are not randomized causal-lift estimates, total platform profit, a guarantee of conversion or proof that an untrained ranker improves deals. Provider costs, customer charges, verified contracts and completed closings retain distinct meanings.

Legacy voice, buyer-search and recovery rollups cover only part of their downstream contact costs. Their historical sums stay visible, but those cohorts cannot promote a shared winner until full cost attribution exists. Safe ordinary exploration continues. This prevents a partial cost total from masquerading as evidence of better contracts per dollar.

## Durable improvement workflow

Recurring operational failures produce versioned proposals. All-age unresolved support cases remain reviewable. Refresh telemetry distinguishes execution success, partial account failures, freshness and due work. The cohort-specific policy lifecycle advances only from canonical release CI, actual immutable assignments and matching policy decisions. It can return allocation to exploration when winner evidence no longer qualifies. See [operations review](learning-operations-review.md).

General support or incident proposals still require a reviewed implementation and offline evidence. The system does not grant itself permission to rewrite code, messages, prices or contact policy.

The separate free synchronization switch only adds existing property IDs to the existing ready list under original quota, lease and receipt checks. Targeting requires complete acknowledged coverage and immutable query binding; synchronization alone is not purchase authorization.

Migration files and executable fixtures are the release's evidence. Record actual release/activation results after verification, without saving contact data, credentials or reconstructed provider receipts.
