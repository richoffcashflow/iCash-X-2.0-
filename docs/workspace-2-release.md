# iCash X 2.0 workspace release

Shipped: branded header/sign-in, paginated account-owned property results, pause/resume and per-property takeover, unsigned purchase/assignment drafts and buyer/title packages, proposed showing requests with overlap checks, funding choices from $20, pacing selection, provisional planning estimates, and versioned purchase-consent receipts. Related records are fetched for the current property page so older saved deals are retained at scale.

Contract documents are printable HTML drafts, not state-approved forms or executed agreements. Contract names come from the authenticated customer's saved legal identity. Showing requests do not send invitations or confirm attendance. Payment consent is evidence of accepted terms, not an identity guarantee or waiver of statutory rights.

Planning estimates use clearly disclosed hypothetical customer prices; they are not provider quotes, configured billing rates, or outcome predictions. Duration controls a daily usage budget; unused funds do not expire because the selected duration ends. No deals or income are forecast without suitable outcome data.

## Launch blockers still requiring implementation or activation

- Production acquisition: verified all-in operation rate configuration, company operating funds, and tenant market/data-rights configuration. Existing keys alone do not supply these values. Current operating budget and credit packs remain disabled.
- Live seller/buyer calls: production channel eligibility checks, durable dispatch and provider event reconciliation, callback execution, and authorized offer/negotiation limits. The existing voice practice/import flow is not a complete live dialer.
- Contract execution: state-specific reviewed templates/addenda, an e-signature provider integration, signer verification and webhook-driven executed-document storage. No signature is inferred from funding acceptance.
- Showings: contact delivery, seller/buyer confirmations, cancellation/rescheduling and calendar integration.
- Buyer disposition: production buyer discovery, qualification, permission-aware outreach and evidence-backed matching.
- Closing: a verified title/escrow workflow or provider integration, verified deposits and funding receipts. No deposit collection or wire instructions are generated by this release.
- Free real-data experience: a licensed, source-dated dataset and activation policy. Fictional examples remain labeled.

Do not enable charges for unavailable acquisition or present these pending operations as completed live activity. No paid provider tests were run. Production build and isolated rollback database checks passed; browser visual verification was not completed because the browser download failed certificate validation.
