# Online contract workflow corrections

Contracts are reviewed and signed online through the existing secure text-link flow. No postal delivery, printing or paper-return workflow is offered. Optional signer email remains for agreement updates. Sending and signing still require the existing deal, recipient, terms, consent and provider checks.

Observed support gaps corrected in this release:

- Contract sending questions lacked deterministic product guidance and could be misrouted by the word “owner.” Replies now use the actual property controls: Documents, Edit agreement, Save draft, Send for signatures and Text contract link.
- Support described the signing invitation as email. The guide, signing panel, outbound and matched inbound seller instructions, and text-analysis instructions now consistently describe online signing by text link.
- An expired-check report led to advice to create/upload a replacement contract. An expired check is not proof the document expired. Missing links and uncertain outcomes stay open while the existing request is checked; they do not authorize duplicate agreements or resends.
- Contract cancellation could be classified as subscription cancellation. The contract controls now receive the correct guidance without changing the subscription.
- Short follow-ups and acknowledgments could lose the contract issue or close unverified signing. Local conversation replays cover navigation, delivery failure, expiration, amendments, all required signers, seller-provided contracts, topic switches and unresolved signature evidence.

The shared delivery guidance does not change frozen provider policy hashes or add SMS/voice tool permissions. Text analysis remains draft/review-only where that was its existing authority. No live customer messages, paid calls, signatures, cancellations or provider retries are performed by this release's tests.

Audit limits: live templates currently support one counterparty signer. Multi-owner agreements still require a suitable reviewed template and every required signer; this change does not remove an owner or claim that multi-owner automation is available. Historical signing holds and provider receipts are retained.
