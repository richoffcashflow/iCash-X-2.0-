# Seller price acceptance with timing questions

The complete-answer evidence guard rejected previously supported replies such as
“Yes. I want to close in 30 days.” The price-only branch now recognizes a bounded
grammar of unconditional affirmations followed by explicit timing preferences or
next-step questions. It retains the entire statement for matching. Only narrow
speech fillers and two repeated false starts are normalized; arbitrary trailing
clauses are never discarded.

This saves acceptance of the exact original server quote and revision. It does
not confirm a closing date, the prepared inspection period, text delivery,
signature, or any funds milestone. Final agreement confirmation uses its unchanged
strict answer grammar and exact terms readback. Conditional prices, changed
amounts, material facts, human requests, withdrawals and unrecognized suffixes
remain held.

Calendar and duration recognition is shared with readbacks so spoken years and
durations are not mistaken for counteroffers. Explicit currency remains a money
amount. Later contradictory price conditions and withdrawals invalidate the prior
answer. Existing persistent original-source holds apply before price acceptance;
explicit new lien or additional-owner statements after agreement cannot be erased
by another generic yes.

Agent statements such as “We cannot buy for $90,000” or “The cash offer is no
longer $90,000” also invalidate prior price and agreement confirmation before the
seller answers again. Unrelated financing, fees and timing negations do not become
price refusals.

Offline verification covers the original interruption regression, complete
compound statements, the actual offer service and its original snapshot/version
save, exact final terms, shared memory and the joint source/SQL agreement path.
Provider I/O is mocked. No SQL migration, production reset, historical replay,
paid test or external contact is part of this change.
